Rules_Summary	Each Round: \n1. Redetermine who Controls each Area (highest total of TroopIcon + ScoutIcon). \n2. Visit the next Area and get the bonus. \n3. Follow the Area's instructions. \n4. You may spend ActionIcon to play CardIcon from your hand (paying its CoinIcon cost). \n5. If a player has all their TroopIcon in Area 4, they win.
