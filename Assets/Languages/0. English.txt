Rules	Rules
Rules_Summary	Each Round: \n1. Choose any TokenIcon (token); create 1 of that token at 1 LevelIcon, then upgrade 1 token of that type to the next LevelIcon. \n2. You may sell 2+ TokenIcon and 2+ CardIcon, all at once, to score CoinIcon. \n\nBetween Rounds:\n3. Remove 1 counter from each token's Twist, if a player chose it. \n4. For each Twist with 0 counters, each player draws 2 CardIcon and follows its instructions. \n5. Add 2 counters back onto those Twists. \n6. First player to reach 20+ CoinIcon wins.
