Rules	Rules
Rules_Summary	Each Round: \n1. Redetermine who Controls each Area (highest total of TroopIcon + ScoutIcon). \n2. All players move to the next Area and get its Travel Bonus. \n3. You may repeatedly spend ActionIcon to play CardIcon from your hand (paying its CoinIcon cost). \n4. Once you've stopped playing, follow the Area's instructions. \n5. If a player has all their TroopIcon in Area 4 (ScoutIcon doesn't matter), they win.
